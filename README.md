📊 AuraTech Sales DASHBOARD | Excel Dashboard | 2025-2026

Overview

This is an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales, profit, and order trends for the year 2025-2026. The dashboard is designed to provide actionable business insights for decision-making and to showcase end-to-end data analysis from data cleaning to visualization.

 🔍 Key Business Questions Answered
- What is the overall sales and profitability?
- Which region and category is driving the most revenue?
- Who are the top-performing salespersons?
- What is the monthly sales trend and seasonality?

 📌 Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** $309.1M
- **Total Profit:** $93.5M
- **Profit Margin:** 30.24%
- **Total Orders:** 1,156

🎛️ Dashboard Features

**1. Interactive Slicers / Filters:**
- Year (2025 / 2026)
- Order Date (Timeline from May 2025 to Aug 2026)
- Category (Accessories, Electronics, Furniture)
- Region (Central, East, North, South, West)

**2. Visualizations:**
- **Sales and Profit by Month:** Dual-axis line chart to track revenue and profit trends & seasonality
- **Sales by Region:** Pie chart for regional contribution analysis
- **Top 5 Salesperson:** Bar chart to identify high performers
- **Sales by Category:** Bar chart to understand product mix

 💡 Key Insights - 2025-26
- **Overall Performance:** The business generated $309.1M in sales with a healthy 30.24% profit margin, showing strong demand.
- **Regional Analysis:** East (29%) and North (26%) are the top-performing regions, contributing 55% of total revenue. West (14%) shows an opportunity for growth.
- **Category Contribution:** Electronics dominates with $222.3M (72% of sales), while Furniture ($68.3M) and Accessories ($26.6M) lag significantly, indicating a need for diversification.
- **Sales Team Performance:** Suresh Raina ($41.5M) is the top performer, followed by Rohan Verma ($40.3M). The top 5 salespeople contribute ~62% of total revenue.
- **Seasonality & Trend:** Sales peaked in March ($34.8M) and July ($33.4M), with a noticeable decline in November-December ($14.3M), indicating a seasonal dip in Q4.

 🛠️ Tools & Functions Used
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Functions:** `SUMIFS`, `XLOOKUP`, `IF`, `Pivot Table`, `Pivot Chart`, `Slicers`, `Timeline`
- **Features:** Data Cleaning, Data Modeling, Interactive Dashboard Design, Conditional Formatting
